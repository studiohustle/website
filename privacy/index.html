Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Studio Hustle is a free game you can play in your web browser. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it to what the game needs to work.

What we collect

  • Your game progress. Your save (money, levels, albums, outfits and settings) is kept on your device and backed up to our servers so it isn't lost.
  • An anonymous account ID. The game creates a random ID for you the first time you play. It isn't linked to your name, phone number or contacts.
  • Your artist name, country and look. You choose these. Other players can see them on leaderboards, labels and profile cards.
  • Your email address, only if you add one. It's used to send you a one-time code so you can restore your save on another device. We don't send marketing email.
  • Feedback you choose to send. If you answer the in-game feedback questions, we store your answers.
  • Basic play statistics. We record which milestones players reach (for example, finishing the tutorial or dropping a first album) to see where the game needs improving. We also count page visits with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies or track you across sites.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, location or microphone.
  • We don't use tracking cookies.

Services we use

Saves, leaderboards and labels are stored with Supabase, our database provider. Page visits are counted with GoatCounter. The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process data only to run these services for us.

Purchases and ads

The web version doesn't charge real money or show ads. When the iPhone and Android apps launch, purchases will go through the App Store or Google Play, and the apps may offer optional rewarded ads. We'll update this policy before then to describe exactly what those services receive.

Age

Studio Hustle is intended for players 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your save and profile while you play. You can wipe your progress any time in Settings with Start a new career. To have your account and backups deleted from our servers, email us from the address you saved with, or include your artist name, and we'll remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and mention major changes in the game or on our Discord.
